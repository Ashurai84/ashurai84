### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

> *"Engineering resilient distributed architectures, modern reactive web ecosystems, and production-ready intelligent AI solutions."*

I am a **Software Engineer & Solutions Developer** with deep expertise across modern Full-Stack engineering, mobile ecosystems, distributed system design, and cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence / LLM pipelines. From crafting polished, micro-interaction rich frontends to architecting high-throughput backend services and machine learning models, I build scalable software that delivers real-world impact.

- 🔭 **Currently Building**: **[Sprint-OS](https://github.com/Ashurai84/Sprint-OS)** — A high-performance academic & productivity command center.
- 🧠 **AI & Research**: Developing agentic workflows, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines, and multimodal voice systems (**Dhwani AI**).
- ⚙️ **Core Philosophy**: Clean architecture, test-driven reliability, modular microservices, and pixel-perfect design.
- 🎯 **Looking to Collaborate On**: Enterprise-grade SaaS products, open-source AI tools, and distributed cloud applications.
- 💬 **Ask Me About**: `React`, `TypeScript`, `Node.js`, `Python`, `Flutter`, `System Design`, `Docker`, & `LLM RAG Systems`.
